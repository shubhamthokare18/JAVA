## 🎮 GAME INSTRUCTIONS

### How to Play
1. Use arrow keys to move snake
2. Eat red food dots to grow
3. Avoid hitting walls
4. Avoid hitting yourself
5. Press SPACE to pause/resume
6. Press SPACE after game over to restart

### Gameplay
- **Moving**: 150ms per move (optimized)
- **Growing**: +1 segment per food eaten
- **Scoring**: +1 point per food eaten
- **Game Over**: Wall or self-collision
- **Pause**: Press SPACE anytime
- **Restart**: Press SPACE after game over
